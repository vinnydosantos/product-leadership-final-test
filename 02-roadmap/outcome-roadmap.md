## Refined roadmap — Meridian Foundations

**Objective:** Make Meridian the workflow foremen trust to resolve job-site blockers and keep crews moving.

**Quarter focus:** Make Meridian a practical daily exchange between field and office, reducing repeated reporting and giving foremen useful information back.

### Now — Three pilot bets

| Strategic bet | OKR connection | Backlog item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| We bet that enabling foremen to explain site issues through annotated photos—and having office teams work from that same record—will reduce repeated reporting across Meridian and group texts. | KR3: Reduce duplicate reporting. | #2 — Clearer field reports |
| We bet that asking foremen for only four essential daily-log inputs, while reusing available project data, will reduce reporting friction enough to earn recurring field use. | KR2: Increase retained field usage. | #4 — Less reporting burden |
| We bet that delivering accurate schedule changes relevant to each foreman’s crew will make Meridian useful for deciding what to do next and earn repeat use beyond submitting reports. | KR2: Increase retained field usage. | #11 — Actionable updates |

### Next — Sequenced, not committed

| Intended outcome | Why after Now | Backlog item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foremen can complete essential reporting without connectivity and trust that their records will synchronize. | Use observed pilot failures to scope offline work. Moves into Now if connectivity blocks pilot use, replacing notifications. | #1 — Reliable offline work |
| Foremen can complete their core tasks without navigating enterprise PM complexity. | Learn which pilot tasks earn repeat use before defining a dedicated mobile experience; coordinate with offline work. | #7 — A focused field experience |
| Foremen can submit a clear, reviewable RFI with less preparation and fewer clarification cycles. | Test AI drafting only after confirming preparation—not waiting for answers—is a meaningful source of delay. | #9 — Clearer, faster RFI preparation |

### Later — Conditional bets, not commitments

| Strategic bet | Evidence needed before investment | Backlog item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Giving subcontractors access to relevant documents will shorten field-to-subcontractor handoffs. | Evidence that access delays resolution, plus capacity for external permissions and onboarding. | #14 — Subcontractor handoffs |
| Connecting weather information to planned work will help foremen avoid preventable crew disruption. | A recurring unmet need for weather-related planning and reliable existing schedule data. | #6 — Weather-aware planning |

### Not this quarter

- **#10 — Custom executive dashboards**
- **#12 — New time-tracking module**
- **#5 — Automated compliance checklists**

Preserve existing enterprise commitments; leadership must explicitly assess the two at-risk renewals.

### Execution guardrails

- **Pilot assumptions:** Office teams act on the shared record. Available project data can replace removed daily-log inputs. Schedule updates are accurate and relevant.
- **Connectivity gate:** If connectivity prevents reliable use of the pilot workflows. Replace schedule notifications in Now with a narrowly scoped offline capability. Keep a maximum of three Now items.
- **Capacity:** Triage the broken Procore integration and include repairs within the ten-person team’s capacity.
- **KR1 gap:** Faster field-confirmed work resumption still requires validated ownership and response practices. Clearer reporting alone does not establish a reduction in resolution time.

*Pilot assumptions and numerical KR baselines remain unvalidated.*

### Refinement record

- **Round 1 — Strategic coherence:** Confirmed all Now bets link to KR2 or KR3; identified that the common field-to-office problem needed clearer framing and that KR1 remains insufficiently supported.
- **Round 2 — Assumptions and claims:** Refined the bets to specify office use of the shared record, reuse of available project data, and accurate crew-relevant updates. Preserved the three Rocks subject to the connectivity gate.

**Result:** The refined Now column identifies the quarter’s problem: excessive reporting effort and fragmented communication, with too little useful information returned to foremen. The resolution-time commitment still requires validation.
